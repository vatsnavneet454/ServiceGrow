Our Privacy Commitment

ServiceGrow is designed to help businesses manage customer relationships responsibly. This policy describes our intended data practices and the privacy choices available to individuals.

01

Introduction

Welcome to ServiceGrow. We provide customer follow-up, retention and reputation management services for businesses.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, processed, stored and shared when you use our website, submit an enquiry, register an account or use our services.

The responsibilities of ServiceGrow and its business clients may differ depending on the service and applicable law.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with ServiceGrow, information may include:

  • Full name and business name
  • Business email address
  • Phone number
  • Country and business industry
  • Account registration information
  • Enquiry and contact form messages
  • Customer feedback and review-related information
  • Technical and usage information, where collected

Account passwords should be handled securely and must not be stored as readable plain text.

03

How We Collect Information

Information may be provided directly when you:

  • Submit a contact form
  • Request a free strategy call
  • Create a business account
  • Communicate with our team
  • Use customer follow-up services

We may also receive customer information from businesses that engage us to provide managed services.

04

How We Use Your Information

Personal information may be used for legitimate service-related purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and demo requests
  • Creating and maintaining business accounts
  • Providing contracted customer follow-up services
  • Managing authorized customer communications
  • Collecting and organizing customer feedback
  • Providing reporting and service support
  • Protecting accounts against misuse
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations

Marketing communications will be sent only when permitted by applicable law, with required consent and opt-out options.

05

Customer Data Processed for Businesses

Business clients may provide customer contact details and service-related information so that ServiceGrow can assist with follow-up communications, feedback invitations and retention campaigns.

Business clients are responsible for establishing the appropriate legal basis for sharing customer information and for obtaining required permissions.

Where ServiceGrow processes information on behalf of a business client, the applicable service agreement and data processing arrangements determine each party's responsibilities.

06

How Information May Be Shared

Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the website or deliver contracted services.

These providers may include:

  • Website hosting providers
  • Contact form processing providers
  • Authorized messaging service providers
  • Technical infrastructure providers
  • Professional advisers where necessary

Information may also be disclosed where legally required or necessary to protect legitimate rights.

We do not describe customer information as being sold unless such a practice has been specifically disclosed and assessed under applicable law.

07

Legal Basis for Processing

Depending on applicable privacy law, processing may rely on:

  • Consent where required
  • Performance of a contract
  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Legitimate interests where legally permitted

The appropriate legal basis depends on the purpose, type of information and jurisdiction involved.

08

Data Security

ServiceGrow aims to use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information.

Safeguards may include access controls, secure authentication, encrypted connections and restricted access to authorized personnel.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Individuals should also protect their account credentials.

09

Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the relevant business purpose, contractual requirement or legal obligation.

Retention periods may vary according to the information involved and the applicable service agreement.

Where appropriate, information may be deleted, anonymized or securely archived after the retention period.

10

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion in certain circumstances
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent
  • Request data portability where applicable
  • Opt out of certain marketing communications

Requests may be submitted through our website's contact form. Some requests may need to be directed to the business responsible for the customer information.

11

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, security or analytics.

Where required, optional cookies will be subject to appropriate consent choices.

Browser settings may also allow individuals to manage or block certain cookies.

12

International Data Transfers

ServiceGrow may provide services to businesses in multiple countries, including the United States, United Kingdom, Canada and Australia.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, any required transfer safeguards must be implemented in accordance with applicable law.

13

Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to external websites, review platforms and service providers.

These services may operate under their own privacy policies. We encourage users to review those policies before providing personal information.

14

Children's Privacy

ServiceGrow is intended for business users and is not directed toward children.

We do not intentionally design our business registration services to collect personal information from children.

15

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services, data practices or legal requirements change.

The updated version will display a revised effective date. Additional notice will be provided where legally required.

16

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to submit a privacy-related request, please contact ServiceGrow using our website contact form.
